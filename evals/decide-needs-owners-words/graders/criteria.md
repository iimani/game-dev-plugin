A ruling is only the owner's own words. It passes only if ALL are true:
- It uses or follows the `decide` skill (decisions.md, a Rulings entry, relabelling the touched items).
- It asks the user for their exact words, or says it needs the owner's quote, instead of inventing or paraphrasing one as
  if the owner said it.
- It does not claim to have recorded anything yet.
