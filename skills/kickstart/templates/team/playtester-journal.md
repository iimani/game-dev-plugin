# Playtester's journal

The regular playtester's own notes. `/game-dev:playtest` passes everything below the line, word for word, after the
controls sheet and the player's guide. After each regular run, the session replaces everything below the line with the
tester's rewrite, exactly as written: at most twenty lines, never corrected, trimmed or added to. A wrong belief stays
in, because it's what that player believes. A game without a player's guide has no use for this file.
