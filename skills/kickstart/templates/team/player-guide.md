# Player's guide

What a player knows after about ten sessions. `/game-dev:playtest` passes everything below the line, word for word, to
a practiced playtester, after the controls sheet. A game without this file runs blind playtests only.

- Write it in a player's words, from play: start from what blind testers came to believe, and correct it against the
  real rules. Nothing a player couldn't have learned by playing: no code name, no number the screen never shows, no plan.
- It describes the game as it was before the change under test. Update it after a change merges, never before the run
  that tests it.
- Keep it under forty lines below the line.

---

**What you're trying to do:** <the goal of a session and of the whole game, as a player would say it.>

**What things do:** <each thing the player meets often, by its on-screen look or name, and what it does.>

**What costs you:** <what hurts, kills or sets the player back, and what it takes away.>

**What usually works:** <the habits a player has by now: the order they do things in, what they save, what they avoid.>

**What you still don't get:** <what a player at ten sessions is still unsure of. Leave it out if there's nothing.>
